Little Racers: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

Little Racers (package com.sundayfortgames.littleracers) is a car game for young children, made by Yves Lau ("we"). This policy explains how the app treats your data. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves a few small files in its private storage on your device:

  • Parental settings: play time limits, play hours and an optional 4-digit parent PIN.
  • Play history: how long the game was played each day, shown to parents in the Usage tab.
  • Game progress and the car's design: levels played, the car's colours, stickers and drawing.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, or when you clear the app's storage in Android settings.

Sharing

We do not share any data with anyone, because we do not receive any.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for young children. It collects no personal information from children or anyone else, shows no advertising, contains no purchases and has no links out of the app except this policy, which is reachable only from the grown-up parent settings area.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
